Restaurant stress vs software development stress.

I recently finished watching The Bear, at least the two seasons that are currently available and it is an excellent show, at least in my humble opinion. On thing that hooked me right away was how they captured the stress of working in a kitchen. I haven't worked in a kitchen in over 25 years but I was instantly reminded of the stress dreams I used to have. Dreams where I was desperately looking for something I needed for the orders I was behind on. Of course I couldn't never find it in the weird dream kitchen layout.

I have never had a stress dream about software development. Instead I would just not sleep or have restless sleep. I would lie awake thinking about the problem I was trying to solve or how I would get my work done in the given deadline. If I did have a stress dream it was never about programming but something else.

Stress in a restaurant is intense, loud, and immediate. Deadlines our measured in hours for prep and then minutes during service. You need get your work done efficiently and be consistent. There is no stopping. If you, or someone else, makes a mistake you just recover as quick as possible and continue on.

On the plus side the stress ends when your shift does. At least it did for me as I was just a line cook. Everything resets when the next shift starts.

There are no resets in software development, at least not daily resets. Nothing resets for months after

No short term resets as deadlines are days, weeks or months. Like a frog slowly getting boiled.

Stress in software development is moderate, quiet, and accumulates over time.
