It doesn’t seem to want to stop snowing in Edmonton for some reason, but that’s not the season I’m talking about… I’m talking about the one that concerns the government taking a certain percentage of your earnings every April.

While walking DD to school last week, she educated me on a fictional [Queen of Fables](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_of_Fables "Queen of Fables") who, for her punishment, was banished to the [US Tax Manual](http://www.irs.gov/irm/ "A Tax Manual or The Tax Manual") for eternity!  I then educated DD on the meaning of the phrase “fate worse than death”!

Happy April 30, everyone!
