Next time your entering data for a software demo try spicing it up and sneak some your famous real or fictional addresses.  Often you can't use the fictional state/province but the street addresses are easy to sneak into a demo, or even better, into your product documentation.  Some ideas to get you thinking:

[1428 Elm Street](http://nightmareonelmstreetfilms.com/1428elmstreet.html) [Madstop](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Nightmare_on_Elm_Street), New England United States

[123 Sesame Street](http://www.sesamestreet.org/)

[1600 Pennsylvania Ave NW](http://www.whitehouse.gov/) Washington, DC United States

[Candy Cane Lane](http://denhartigh.dyndns.org/~keesdenhartigh/cclane/) [North Pole](http://www.writetosanta.ca/santas-address.php) H0H 0H0 Canada
