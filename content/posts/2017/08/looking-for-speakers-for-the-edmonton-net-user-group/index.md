[![Edmonton .NET User Group Logo](images/EdmontonNETUserGroupLogo.webp)](https://nftb.saturdaymp.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/EdmontonNETUserGroupLogo.jpeg)Are you passionate about software development and want to share your passion with others?  If so then the [Edmonton .NET Users Group](https://www.meetup.com/Edmonton-NET-User-Group/) is looking for you. Specifically as the newly elected [Program Director](https://www.meetup.com/Edmonton-NET-User-Group/pages/23883329/Executive_Team_2017_-_2018/) I'm looking for you to speak at one of our meetups.

Already a member?  That's even better.  Don't hold back, share with the group.

You don't have be a world-renowned speaker.  In fact, the .NET Users Group is a friendly and supportive environment for first time speakers.  The biggest thing we ask for is passion and willingness to share your knowledge.

Talks can be about anything you like as long it is related to software development in some form.  It also helps if the talk is related to .NET or Windows.  If you, or someone you know, is interested in presenting please let me know via [e-mail](mailto:chris.cumming@saturdaymp.com) or contact me via [Meetup](https://www.meetup.com/Edmonton-NET-User-Group/members/111468412/) (click on my picture).

P.S. - Not interested in speaking but want to connect with passionate, knowledgeable developers?  Join the [Edmonton .NET User Group](https://www.meetup.com/Edmonton-NET-User-Group/) or your friendly local development group.
