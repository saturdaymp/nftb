[![Edmonton .NET User Group Logo](images/EdmontonNETUserGroupLogo.webp)](https://nftb.saturdaymp.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/08/EdmontonNETUserGroupLogo.jpeg)The first [Edmonton .NET User Group (EDMUG)](https://www.meetup.com/preview/Edmonton-NET-User-Group) talk of the 2017-2018 season is this Monday, September 18th.  The talk is by [Shenyi Bao](https://www.isaveutime.com/) and is titled Web Site Performance Tuning.  All the details can be found [here](https://www.meetup.com/preview/Edmonton-NET-User-Group/events/242896361).

Please also don't forget about the EDMUG survey.  If you are part of the EDMUG group, or are thinking of joining, please take a couple minutes to fill out the [survey](https://www.surveymonkey.com/r/5PKRNLB).

Finally we are always looking for engaging and passionate speakers for the EDMUG meetups.  If you, or someone you know, would like to present please let us know by e-mailing [chris.cumming@saturdaymp.com](chris.cumming@saturdaymp.com) or [info@edmug.net](info@edmug.net).
